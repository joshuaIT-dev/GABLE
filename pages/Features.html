Features That Transform Learning

Gable combines gaming mechanics with educational excellence to create an unmatched learning experience

COMPETE

Battle Quiz Modes

Compete in Team Battle (5v5), Solo duels (1v1, on-class), or Battle Royale (up to 50-60+ players). Real-time competition makes learning thrilling.

  • Live matchmaking with classmates
  • Real-time leaderboards
  • XP rewards for victories
PROGRESS

XP & Rewards System

Earn XP for every correct answer. Perfect quizzes (100%) earn 150 XP. Redeem 350 XP for a +1 grade boost on any quiz.

  • Convert performance into rewards
  • Build perfect streaks for bonus XP
  • Strategic grade boosting
PROGRESS

Advanced Analytics

Track performance across formal and informal activities. Visualize strengths, weaknesses, and improvement trends over time.

  • Subject-wise performance breakdown
  • Formal vs informal comparison
  • Progress tracking over time
TEACH

Syllabus Alignment

Instructors earn teaching XP based on quiz-syllabus alignment. High-quality content is rewarded, ensuring curriculum relevance.

  • Automated alignment scoring
  • Quality incentives for educators
  • Curriculum relevance tracking
COMPETE

Live Leaderboards

Compete for the top spot in class, section, and school-wide leaderboards. Rankings reset each semester for fair competition.

  • Class & school-wide rankings
  • Seasonal resets for fairness
  • Achievement badges
TEACH

Quiz Builder

Instructors can build rich quizzes with multiple question types, timers, and difficulty settings tailored to any subject.

  • Multiple question formats
  • Difficulty & timer controls
  • Instant student feedback

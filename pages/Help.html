Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I lose a battle quiz?
Losing a battle quiz still earns you partial XP based on how many questions you answered correctly. You won't lose existing XP.
How does matchmaking work in battle modes?
Matchmaking pairs you with classmates in the same section or course. Team Battle matches 5 players per side, Solo is 1v1, and Battle Royale can have up to 50-60+ players.
Can I use XP boosts on formal quizzes?
XP boosts can be redeemed on both formal and informal quizzes, but grade boost redemptions apply only to formal quiz scores, subject to instructor approval.
What if my instructor's quiz has errors?
Use the "Report Question" button during a quiz. Your instructor will be notified and can issue score corrections. Admins review flagged content regularly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I lose a battle quiz?
Losing a battle quiz still earns you partial XP based on how many questions you answered correctly. You won't lose existing XP.
How does matchmaking work in battle modes?
GABLE's public feature guide describes matchmaking with classmates. Team Battle is 5v5, Solo Duel is 1v1 (on-class), and Battle Royale supports up to 50-60+ players. The available mode depends on the quiz assigned to your section; check its instructions or ask your instructor.
Can I use XP boosts on formal quizzes?
Grade boosts apply to fully graded formal quiz results below 100%. Each boost costs 150 XP and adds 1 percentage point; you can boost the same result repeatedly up to 100%. You may also use an available free grade-boost token. Grade boosts must be enabled by the administrator.
What if my instructor's quiz has errors?
Use the "Report Question" option during a quiz to tell your instructor about the issue. If you have a concern about your score, contact your instructor for help.

Troubleshooting

  • Quiz won't start

    Check your connection and quiz availability. If an active quiz is open, avoid refreshing because it uses another opening and the timer keeps running.

  • XP not updating

    XP is awarded after grading is complete. Identification answers may need instructor review.

  • Can't find my section

    Contact your instructor to be added to the correct section

  • Login not working

    Check that you selected the correct role and entered your account details. Contact support if the problem continues.

  • Leaderboard not loading

    Check your connection and reload the Leaderboard tab. If it still does not load, contact support.

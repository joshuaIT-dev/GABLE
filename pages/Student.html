A QUICK GUIDE

How can we help?

Find your next step in the student workspace.

01

Take a quiz

Open My Quizzes to see formal and informal activities assigned to your section. Choose a card to read the instructions and start.

02

Understand your results

Multiple-choice answers can be graded automatically. Identification answers wait for instructor review. Performance shows your graded scores and completion.

03

Unlock and use power-ups

Complete assigned quizzes to earn semester XP. Rewards unlock once at these milestones: 300 XP: profile frame; 700: Streak Freeze; 1,200: 50/50; 2,000: 1.5x XP; 3,000: discounted hints; 4,500: free grade boost; 6,500: Master Crown. Spending XP does not lower your level.

Open Power-Ups for your remaining XP, saved uses and exact steps. Use 50/50 before answering a four-choice question. XP boosts apply to results earning base XP above zero. A grade boost needs a graded formal result below 100% with no previous boost, plus a token or 350 XP and enabled administrator settings.

04

Get help studying

Ask Study Buddy to explain a concept or guide your review. Open the full tab for more space, or use the floating chat while browsing.

Already own a reward? These three apply automatically.
  • 1.5× XP: Finish a quiz that earns more than zero base XP. One use applies when the result receives its XP award; pending results wait for grading.
  • 50% off hints: Choose Hint before answering a question. You need 25 spendable XP and a remaining discount. One successful hint uses both; it is not free.
  • Streak Freeze: Miss one day, then submit a quiz the following day. One freeze protects that missed day. Logging in alone does not count; longer gaps are not covered.

No activation button is needed. Check Power-Ups → Your Power-Up Inventory for uses left. Used-up rewards stop applying.

Waiting for a grade?

A result marked Awaiting review needs your instructor's feedback. Your final score and XP will appear after grading.
